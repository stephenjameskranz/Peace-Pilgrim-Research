## 3. Broken, missing or swapped links

- Parts of these entries (usually the words "(with photo)" or a second half of the title) also link to the placeholder: 1953 Jan 12 Fullerton; 1953 California; 1953 Guthrie OK; 1953 Oklahoma City "Pilgrim Hikes to City" and "5000-Mile Stroll"; 1953 Ap 13 Peacemaker; 1953 St. Louis MO; 1953 June 25 Newton KS; 1953 Chicago Sun Times; 1953 Aug 25 and Aug 26 Kalamazoo; 1953 Nov 30 Wilmington DE; 1953 Dec 3 Cecil MD; 1953 Washington DC; 1953 Dec 16 Newark NJ.

Other link problems:

- **1953 Sept 17 – Toledo OH (two entries):** the two links are swapped. "Woman Hiking Across US In Plea For World Peace" opens the Toledo Blade article and "Peace Pilgrim in Toledo She's Hiking 5000" opens the Toledo Times article. The two files are named `PPclip_1953Sept17-ToledoOh-w-photo.pdf` and `...ToledoOH-w-photo.pdf`, differing only by capital letters, which also makes them collide when downloaded on Windows or Mac. Renaming one would help.
- **1960 Dec 12 – Fort Worth TX:** links to a second copy of the Tyler TX Dec 3 article. The Fort Worth article itself is not linked.
- **1953 Nov 30 – Wilmington DE: Pilgrim of Peace Here on Long Hike:** links to the Morning News file used by the entry above it.
- **1953 Aug 25 – Peace Pilgrim Walking Coast to Coast** and **1953 Aug 25 – Kalamazoo MI: Walking from Coast to Coast for Peace** link to the same file; likewise the two **1953 Aug 26 Kalamazoo** entries and the two **1953 Chicago** entries (Aug 5 Chicago Sun-Times).
- **1953 – Ag: Peace on Cross-Country Walk Against War:** same article as the St. Louis entry; "Ag" is unexplained.
